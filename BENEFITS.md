# Benefits

## User benefits

- One first-party place for files that is designed around ownership, privacy, portability, and understandable sharing.
- A product model that can support both convenient collaborative storage and a future stronger client-side encrypted privacy mode.
- Clear recovery and lifecycle language that does not misrepresent Trash, sync, CI, or encryption as guarantees they are not.

## Administrator benefits

- Ownership-first relational model designed for multi-user isolation from the beginning.
- Separation of durable payloads, metadata, temporary transfer state, derived data, configuration, and backups.
- Local-first observability and safe defaults without mandatory remote telemetry.
- Versioned API and documented boundaries that support future GoreeCloud Suite integrations.
- Migration and export direction intended to prevent application lock-in.

## GoreeCloud platform benefits

Drive becomes the authoritative file-cloud experience while Sync remains the transfer engine, reducing duplicated responsibility and allowing both products to evolve independently behind explicit contracts.
