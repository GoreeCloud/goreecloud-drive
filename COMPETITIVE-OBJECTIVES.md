# Competitive Objectives

GoreeCloud Drive benchmarks mature file-cloud experiences including Google Drive, Proton Drive, Dropbox, and Nextcloud without copying their branding or becoming dependent on any one vendor.

## Capabilities to match or exceed

- Fast, understandable file and folder management across web, desktop, and mobile.
- Personal storage plus durable shared team/family Spaces.
- Fine-grained roles, revocable sharing, expiring links, passphrase protection, and file requests.
- Reliable large-file transfer with pause, resume, integrity verification, conflict handling, and offline behavior.
- Version history, Trash, activity, favorites, search, previews, quotas, and portable export.
- First-party APIs and interoperable clients.

## Areas GoreeCloud intends to exceed

- Ownership and self-hosting control without advertising or behavioral monetization.
- Separation of administrator privilege from automatic private-content read authority.
- Explicit Standard Spaces and future client-side encrypted Private Spaces.
- Storage portability independent of the application database and runtime.
- Deep first-party integration with Identity, Sync, Search, Network, Notify, Manager, Photos, Documents, Everkeep, and Backup.
- Evidence-based security, privacy, recovery, and lifecycle claims.
- Migration Mode with dry runs, checksums, resumability, validation, and rollback/source-retention guidance.

## Intentionally rejected

- Ad-supported storage.
- Dark patterns that make export, self-hosting, account deletion, or migration intentionally difficult.
- Mandatory third-party analytics for ordinary operation.
- Unsupported claims that encryption branding alone proves zero knowledge.
- Treating version history or sync as a backup.
